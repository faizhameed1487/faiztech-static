What to Look for in a best Laptop Under $300

  • RAM: 8GB for Windows 11. 4GB is okay only for Chromebooks and very light use.
  • Storage: 128GB minimum. SSD or UFS is much faster than eMMC.
  • Display: Full HD (1080p) IPS if possible. Avoid 720p-class HD panels for long study sessions.
  • Battery: 8+ hours of real-world use.
  • Processor: Intel Core i3 / Ryzen 3 or newer for Windows. A Celeron is fine for Chromebooks and basic tasks.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are laptops under $300 good enough in 2026?

Yes. In 2026, $300 can get you a Full HD display, 8GB RAM and SSD storage, which is plenty for students and everyday users.

Should I buy a Chromebook or a Windows laptop under $300?

If you mostly use Google apps, YouTube and web tools, a Chromebook gives better battery and speed for the price. If you need desktop Microsoft Office or specific Windows software, choose a Windows laptop.

What is the minimum RAM for a laptop in 2026?

8GB is the recommended minimum for smooth Windows 11 use. 4GB is acceptable only for Chromebooks or very basic tasks.

Are refurbished laptops safe to buy?

Yes, if you buy certified refurbished from a reputable seller with at least a 90-day warranty. Business laptops like the Dell Latitude and Lenovo ThinkPad are especially good refurbished buys.

Which is the best budget laptop for students under $300?

The Acer Aspire Go 15 suits most students: Full HD display, 8GB RAM, a comfortable keyboard and 10+ hours of battery. For Chromebook fans, the ASUS Chromebook CX1 is the top pick.
